What I do

Every automated line runs the same loop. I work on all four stages of it, on real production equipment.

Control loopStages 4

  1. Sense

    Stage 1

    Cognex and Keyence cameras check every part, and Balluff IO-Link sensors track vibration, temperature, humidity and air pressure.

    • Cognex
    • Keyence
    • iRVision
    • Balluff IO-Link

  2. Decide

    Stage 2

    Allen-Bradley and Siemens PLC logic decides what runs next. Safety PLCs and poka-yoke error proofing decide when it stops.

    • Studio 5000
    • RSLogix
    • Siemens
    • Safety PLC

  3. Act

    Stage 3

    FANUC and Universal Robots, VFD-driven motors and pneumatics do the work. I integrate them and tune the cycle time.

    • FANUC
    • UR
    • VFDs
    • Pneumatics

  4. Monitor

    Stage 4

    Ignition and FactoryTalk View screens for alarms and diagnostics, linked to MES for traceability and uptime tracking.

    • Ignition 8.1
    • FactoryTalk View
    • MES
    • EtherNet/IP

Then I close the loop: I/O checkout, commissioning, startup, and root-cause fixes that keep uptime high.
